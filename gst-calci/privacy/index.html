GST CALCI · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective 31 July 2026

In short: GST Calci works offline and has no account, advertisements, analytics or cloud storage. Calculation history and preferences remain on your device unless you choose to share or export information.

Information we collect

Nirvana Solutions does not collect, receive, sell or share your calculation data or device information through GST Calci. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Information stored locally

Your calculation history and preferences are stored only on your device. Local preferences may include your selected layout, GST rate, inclusive or exclusive mode, CGST + SGST or IGST selection, favourites, rounding and other display settings.

Sharing and exports

Calculation information leaves the app only when you choose an action such as Share, Copy or Export. GST Calci can share a calculation summary with a branded image and can export history as a CSV file. The destination app or service you select handles that information under its own privacy terms.

Feedback and problem reports

When you choose Feedback or Report a problem, GST Calci opens a pre-filled email draft in your email app. Nothing is sent automatically. You can review, change or discard the message before sending it. If you voluntarily send the email, Nirvana Solutions receives your sender address and included message, uses them only to respond or provide support, and does not sell them. Your email provider handles the email under its own terms.

Retention and deletion

Calculation history remains on your device until you delete individual entries, clear the history or remove the app. Preferences remain until you change them, clear the app's data or remove the app. Shared and exported information remains wherever you chose to send or save it until you delete it there.

Government affiliation and calculation guidance

GST Calci is an independent utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or an official application of the Government of India or any tax authority. The app provides calculation assistance only. You should verify the GST rate and tax treatment applicable to each supply.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
