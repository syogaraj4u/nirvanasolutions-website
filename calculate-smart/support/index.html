CALCULATE SMART · HELP CENTRE

Support

App name on your device

The App Store product is named Calculate Smart. After installation, its icon is labelled Calculate+.

Edit any part of a calculation

Tap or drag inside the expression to place the cursor. Select digits or operators to replace, copy, cut or paste them. Hold backspace to delete continuously. An invalid part may be highlighted while an expression is unfinished; complete or edit that part to continue.

Switch calculators and converters

Use the calculator title or navigation menu to switch between Standard, Scientific, Fractions, Percentage, Currency and Unit Converter. Calculator and unit-conversion features work without an internet connection.

Use currency reference rates

Open Currency and tap refresh to request the latest available Frankfurter reference rates. The app downloads only public exchange-rate and supported-currency data; it does not send the amount you entered or your calculation history. The newest successfully downloaded rates are saved on your device for offline use. Rates are estimates and may differ from banks, cards or currency exchanges.

View and manage history

Open History to search previous calculations, reopen and edit an entry, pin important results, add notes or tags, delete entries, or export a copy. History is stored locally. You can disable history saving, clear history or reset the app in Settings.

Copy, share or export

Copy and Share use Apple system features. Information leaves Calculate Smart only when you choose a destination. That destination app or service handles the information under its own privacy terms.

Accessibility and keyboard help

Calculate Smart supports VoiceOver, larger text, increased contrast and reduced motion. On iPad and supported Mac layouts, you can also use number keys, operators, Return, Escape, Delete and arrow-key cursor movement.

A result does not look correct

  • Check the selected calculator mode and number-format setting.
  • Finish any missing number or parenthesis and review the highlighted part.
  • For Currency, check the displayed rate date and refresh when online.
  • Remember that division by zero cannot produce a result.
