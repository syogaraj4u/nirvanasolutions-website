ONE BILL · GST BILLING

All accounts.
One place.

One Bill is a focused iPhone billing workspace for managing eight firms, producing premium GST invoice PDFs, preparing NIC-structured exports and closing each month cleanly.

Built for daily billing

  • Company-wise sales, buyers, products and credit notes
  • Searchable global buyer and product masters
  • Premium invoice PDFs with Bill To, Ship To and total quantity
  • IMEI, serial and colour paste from spreadsheets
  • NIC-structured e-Invoice and e-Way Bill JSON preparation
  • Monthly reconciliation and accountant ZIP export
  • Local compliance checks, app lock and protected storage

Private by design

One Bill is an invite-only workspace that uses Sign in with Apple to verify approved access. It has no advertising, tracking, analytics or developer-operated billing cloud in this release. Business records remain on the device unless the user explicitly exports or shares a file.
