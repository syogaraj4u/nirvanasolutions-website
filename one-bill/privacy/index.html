ONE BILL · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026

In short: One Bill has no account, advertisements, analytics, tracking or developer-operated cloud service. Billing records remain on the device unless the user explicitly exports or shares a file.

Information we collect

Nirvana Solutions does not collect, receive, sell or share personal data, billing data, company records, buyer records, product records, invoices, credit notes, IMEI numbers, serial numbers or usage history through One Bill.

Local storage

Companies, buyers, products, documents, audit events, preferences and monthly-close information are stored in One Bill's local app container. This release does not synchronize them to a Nirvana Solutions server.

Files and sharing

Content leaves One Bill only when the user chooses an action such as Share PDF, export JSON, export spreadsheet or create an accountant ZIP. iOS sends the file only to the destination selected by the user. The receiving app or service handles it under its own privacy policy.

Device permissions

Apple's Local Authentication framework may be used to lock the app; One Bill does not receive or store biometric data. Camera or photo access is requested only when a user starts a feature that requires it. Permissions remain under the user's control in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

Users control their local records. Drafts and eligible master records can be deleted in the app. Removing One Bill removes its local app data, subject to device backups and files the user previously exported elsewhere.

Children

One Bill is a business utility and is not directed specifically to children.

Changes

If data practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated before the relevant app release.
