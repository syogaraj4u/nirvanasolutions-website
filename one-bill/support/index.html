ONE BILL · HELP CENTRE

Support

Sign in or explore the fictional demo

Approved users can sign in with Apple using their invited identity. App reviewers and prospective users can open the fictional demo from the sign-in screen; demo records are not connected to any real company and changes are discarded.

Choose or change a company

Choose a company on first launch. Inside the app, use the company control at the top of a main screen to switch. Pin frequently used companies so they appear first.

Create a sales bill

Open Sales, tap the add button, choose the buyer and product, enter the required quantity and whole-rupee price, then review and finalize. The final invoice number is allocated only at finalization.

Paste IMEI or serial data

Open IMEI / serial numbers on an invoice item and paste spreadsheet rows. One Bill supports IMEI only, IMEI with serial, IMEI with serial and colour, serial only, and serial with colour. Two IMEI values can be entered for one unit where required.

Preview or share an invoice

Open a finalized bill and choose the PDF preview action. Review the Bill To, Ship To, quantity and totals before sharing the PDF.

Prepare NIC files

Complete the mandatory GST and transport information, then use the document export actions. The NIC Compliance Centre validates envelope, version and required-field structure against the bundled schemas. One Bill prepares files; final statutory submission remains the user's responsibility.

Export a month for the accountant

Open Reports, choose the company and month, review reconciliation and export the accountant package. Close the month only after drafts and compliance gaps are resolved; an administrator can reopen it if a correction is necessary.

Back up important records

This release does not include cloud synchronization. Export and retain accountant packages and other important files in a secure destination chosen by your business.
