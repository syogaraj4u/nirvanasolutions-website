SMS FORWARDER · ANDROID · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026

In short: The direct version of SMS Forwarder accesses new incoming SMS only after the user gives clear consent and Android permission. It sends those messages directly from the phone to destinations the user configures. The current app has no advertisements, analytics, tracking, account requirement, or Nirvana Solutions-operated message relay. The permission-free demo version does not access incoming SMS.

Who we are

SMS Forwarder is an Android application developed by Nirvana Solutions in India. This policy applies to the direct and demo distributions of SMS Forwarder.

SMS data the direct app accesses

When live forwarding is enabled, the direct app processes the following information from each new incoming SMS:

  • the sender identifier or phone number;
  • the message text;
  • the received date and time; and
  • the SIM slot when Android makes it available.

This access enables the app's core forwarding feature, including sender, keyword, SIM, privacy, and destination rules. SMS Forwarder does not import the historical SMS inbox and does not collect messages from another phone where the app is not installed. The current scope does not include MMS attachments, RCS history, contacts, call logs, location, camera, microphone, or reply-to-SMS functionality.

How messages are used and transmitted

After the user enables forwarding, SMS Forwarder can operate in the background when the app is not open. It applies the user's rules and transmits matching SMS data only to destinations the user has configured and enabled.

  • Email: the phone connects directly to the user's configured email provider over SSL/TLS or STARTTLS and sends a separate message to each enabled email destination.
  • HTTPS destinations: the phone can send directly to user-provided Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord, or custom webhook endpoints over HTTPS.
  • Another phone or WhatsApp: these remain user-confirmed sharing actions. SMS Forwarder does not silently automate the consumer WhatsApp app.

Destination providers receive forwarded content under their own terms and privacy policies. Nirvana Solutions does not choose or control the user's recipients, mail provider, workspace, or webhook service.

Permissions and user control

The direct app requests Android's incoming-SMS permission only after an in-app disclosure and affirmative consent. It uses internet access to reach configured destinations and may request notification permission to show privacy-safe forwarding and health alerts. Notifications do not contain the SMS sender or message body.

Live forwarding is off by default. The user can decline permission, keep forwarding off, disable individual destinations, change rules, revoke permission in Android Settings, or delete all local app data. The demo build does not request incoming-SMS access.

Local storage and security

Destinations, endpoint addresses, SMTP credentials, queued SMS, failed-message content, rules, and the latest privacy-processed SMS are encrypted on the device using AES-256-GCM with a non-exportable Android Keystore key. App backups and device transfer are disabled for these app data domains.

SMTP app passwords are stored encrypted and are used only to authenticate directly with the configured email provider. SMS Forwarder does not ask for the user's normal email password. Passwords, endpoint URLs, message bodies, senders, tokens, and encryption keys are excluded from exported support reports.

Retention and deletion

  • Queued SMS content is deleted after routing finishes.
  • A failed message may remain encrypted for up to 24 hours so the user can retry or delete it, after which it is automatically removed.
  • The latest received SMS is replaced by the next one and is removed when the user deletes local app data.
  • Local activity keeps a limited set of destination name, channel, outcome, time, and sanitized diagnostic entries. It does not keep the SMS sender or message body.
  • Removing the app or using Delete all local data removes locally held app information. Copies already accepted by an email or third-party destination remain under that provider's retention controls.

Data collected by Nirvana Solutions

The current app does not send SMS content, credentials, destination addresses, identifiers, or usage analytics to Nirvana Solutions. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or operate a message relay for this version. If a future version introduces an optional developer-operated cloud service, the feature, consent flow, Play data-safety declaration, and this policy will be updated before that service is used.

Support communications

If the user voluntarily contacts support, Nirvana Solutions receives the sender address and information the user chooses to include. This information is used to respond or investigate the request. Users should not include SMS content, app passwords, endpoint secrets, OTPs, or banking information in support messages.

Safety and responsible use

SMS may contain OTPs, payment alerts, banking information, private conversations, or other sensitive content. Users should install and configure SMS Forwarder only for themselves or with lawful authorization, and only with destinations they control. A bank, government office, courier, employer, or other organization should not ask a user to install this app or forward verification codes. If another person directs the setup, stop and verify the request independently.

Children

SMS Forwarder is a general utility and is not directed to children. The app should not be used to monitor another person's messages without their knowledge and lawful authorization.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, the revised policy and effective date will be published on this page. A new in-app consent will be requested when required by the nature of the change or applicable platform policy.
