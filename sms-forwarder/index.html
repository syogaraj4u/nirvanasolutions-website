Nirvana Solutions product · Android alpha

New messages.
Your destinations.

SMS Forwarder routes new incoming SMS from an Android phone to multiple destinations selected and controlled by the user. It is free, private by design and built around clear consent.

Explore features ↗Read privacy policy

Availability: private Android alpha. Google Play review is planned; this page does not represent a public Play Store release.

Dependable forwarding without a relay subscription.

The direct build processes new SMS on the phone and sends each matching message separately to every enabled destination. It can continue in the background after setup; the app does not need to remain open.

Message content and credentials are protected locally using Android Keystore-backed encryption. Delivery is direct to the providers configured by the user—Nirvana Solutions does not operate a message relay for this version.

Multiple destinations

Deliver independently to multiple email inboxes, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord or custom HTTPS webhooks.

Smart routing

Choose sender, keyword and SIM conditions so only the intended messages follow each route.

Guided health

See one clear next action for permission, authentication, background delivery, network or provider issues.

Reliable retries

Temporary failures enter an encrypted retry queue while one failed destination does not block the others.

Private by design

No advertisements, analytics, tracking, account requirement or developer-operated message relay.

Clear consent

First-run safety information and a prominent SMS disclosure explain access before Android permission is requested.

Current scope

SMS Forwarder handles new incoming SMS only. It does not import message history, send replies, forward MMS or RCS content, or silently automate consumer WhatsApp.
