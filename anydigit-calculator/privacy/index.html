ANYDIGIT CALCULATOR · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 August 2026

In short: AnyDigit Calculator has no advertisements, account system, analytics or tracking. Calculations and history stay on your device. Only the Currency Converter uses the internet, to request reference exchange rates.

Information Nirvana Solutions collects

Nirvana Solutions does not collect, receive, sell or share personal information, calculations, clipboard contents, calculation history, advertising identifiers, device identifiers or usage analytics through AnyDigit Calculator. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

Your calculation history, unfinished expression, cached currency rates, favourites and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can disable history saving, delete individual entries, clear all history or reset the app from Settings. Removing the app may also remove its local data.

Currency-rate requests

When you refresh or use the Currency Converter, the app can contact the public Frankfurter service at api.frankfurter.dev to obtain supported currencies and reference exchange rates. The request does not include the amount you entered, your calculations, history, clipboard contents, account information or an app-specific identifier. As with any internet request, the service and network providers can receive technical connection information such as an IP address under their own policies. The most recent rates are cached so conversion can continue offline.

Copying, sharing and exporting

Information leaves the app only when you deliberately copy, share or export it. Apple’s system share sheet sends the selected content to the destination you choose. That destination handles the information under its own privacy terms. AnyDigit Calculator does not automatically attach calculations or history to feedback messages.

Support email

If you email support, Nirvana Solutions receives the sender address and information you choose to include. It is used only to respond to your request and is not sold. Your email provider handles the message under its own terms.

Children’s privacy

AnyDigit Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated with a revised effective date.
