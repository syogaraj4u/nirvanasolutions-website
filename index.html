Questions & answers

Clear answers.
Before we begin.

Every engagement is shaped around the problem, but these are the questions we answer most often.

Ask something else ↗
What kinds of projects do you build?+

We build websites, web applications, custom business software, iOS and Android apps, AI automation and ongoing software maintenance.

How long does a software project take?+

Timing depends on scope and complexity. After a short discovery conversation, we provide a phased plan with clear milestones and an estimated delivery timeline.

Can we start with a small prototype or MVP?+

Yes. We can begin with a focused audit, prototype or minimum viable product, then expand once the direction is proven.

Do you provide support after launch?+

Yes. We provide maintenance, monitoring, improvements and technical support after launch through an agreed support plan.

How is project pricing decided?+

Pricing is based on scope, complexity, integrations and delivery needs. Suitable projects can use fixed milestones, while evolving work can use a monthly engagement.

Can you improve an existing website, app or software?+

Yes. We can review what you already have, identify the highest-impact gaps, fix urgent issues and continue with structured improvements or maintenance.

Do you work with clients outside India?+

Yes. We work remotely with clients worldwide and agree a practical communication rhythm, milestones and review process before work begins.

What do I need before contacting you?+

A short description of the problem, who will use the product and the result you want is enough to begin. You do not need a formal specification.
