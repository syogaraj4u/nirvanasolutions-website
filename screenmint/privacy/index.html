SCREENMINT · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 August 2026

In short: ScreenMint has no account, advertisements, analytics, tracking or developer-operated cloud service. Recordings, editing projects, preferences and protected-vault information remain on your device unless you explicitly export or share a file.

Information we collect

Nirvana Solutions does not collect, receive, sell or share your recordings, audio, photos, videos, editing content, device identifiers, usage history or precise location through ScreenMint.

Screen and audio recording

ScreenMint uses Apple ReplayKit and its broadcast extension only after you explicitly start a recording through the iOS system interface. A visible system indication is shown while recording. Depending on your selected mode and what iOS permits, a recording may contain the screen, system audio, microphone audio or both. Some protected media and apps can block video or audio capture.

Camera, microphone, Photos and Face ID

  • Microphone: used only when you choose commentary, microphone audio or the microphone level test.
  • Camera: used only when you open the optional camera preview or a camera-supported feature.
  • Photos: used only to import a video you select or save an export when you request it.
  • Face ID or device authentication: used by Apple's Local Authentication framework to unlock protected recordings. ScreenMint does not receive or store biometric data.
Permission status is processed by iOS and remains under your control in device Settings.

Local storage and crash recovery

Recordings, segments, imported videos, edited exports, folders, favourites and preferences are stored in ScreenMint's local app or App Group containers. Crash-safe segments may remain temporarily so an interrupted recording can be recovered. The app does not upload this content to a Nirvana Solutions server.

Sharing, Files and exports

Content leaves ScreenMint only when you choose an action such as Share, Save to Photos, Save to Files or another export destination. The receiving app or service handles the exported content under its own privacy policy. Metadata cleanup can remove unnecessary metadata during supported exports, but ScreenMint cannot control metadata later added by another service.

Retention and deletion

Local recordings remain until you delete them, clear the app's data or remove the app. Items deleted in ScreenMint are removed from its storage. Files you previously exported remain in the selected destination until you delete them there. Removing ScreenMint does not delete copies already saved to Photos, Files or another app.

Feedback and support

If you voluntarily email support, Nirvana Solutions receives your sender address and the information you include. It is used only to respond or investigate the issue and is not sold. Your email provider processes the message under its own terms.

Children and sensitive recordings

ScreenMint is a general utility and is not directed specifically to children. Recording may capture personal or confidential information visible or audible on the device. You are responsible for obtaining necessary consent and for reviewing content before sharing it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be published on this page.
