Nirvana Solutions product

Capture inventory
in a scan.

IMEI Barcode Scanner turns device labels and product barcodes into organised, reviewable inventory records—without uploading scan data.

Faster intake. Cleaner records. Less typing.

Use your phone camera to capture IMEI numbers, serial numbers and supported product barcodes while receiving, checking or dispatching stock. Add model, colour, quantity and session details, then review everything before export.

The scanner is built for privacy and unreliable connectivity. Records stay on the device, the core workflow works offline and data leaves only when you choose to copy, share, export or back it up.

Fast barcode capture

Scan supported labels with the camera and reduce manual entry errors.

Organised sessions

Group scans into clear inventory sessions with editable product details.

Flexible exports

Review, copy, share or export records when the session is complete.

Offline and private

No account, advertising, analytics or automatic cloud uploads.
